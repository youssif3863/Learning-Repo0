Page references

File 2 (4_5875397832227168769(1).PDF) holds printed pp. 197–433, where printed page = PDF viewer page + 196; its built-in viewer labels restart at 1 and should not be used.
  • Printed p. 197 appears in both files. Chapter 15 (Colorectal Cancer, pp. 195–209) spans the join and is treated as one chapter.
  • Source pages (GIT Internal Med, GIT Others, Hepatology) are the printed page numbers of those files, which equal their PDF pages.
  • The main book does not mark a mid-module boundary. Part 1 (Chapters 1–15) and Part 2 (Chapters 16–40) follow the scope of the Before- and After-Mid-Module handouts.

What the integration did

  • {{N_ENTRIES}} teaching additions in {{N_CH_WITH}} chapters: {{N_IM}} Internal Medicine, {{N_PHARM}} Pharmacology, {{N_PATH}} Pathology, {{N_MICRO}} Microbiology and {{N_HEP}} Hepatology. Together they carry {{N_ROWS}} original ledger rows. {{N_CH_EMPTY}} chapters have no additions and appear as short entries.
  • Points that taught the same topic from the same book page were combined into one entry, with every distinct fact and every page reference kept. The three handouts contained no true duplicates of each other: where two touch the same topic (for example, the H. pylori → cancer sequence in Chapters 3 and 4), each keeps its own facts and they refer to each other.
  • One misfiled point (Hepatitis G, book p. 249) was moved from Chapter 19 to Chapter 18, where that page belongs.
  • Sentences that an independent check found already on the cited book pages were trimmed. Two values that the source handouts printed as settled but that the book contradicts were moved out of the teaching points into review: the Hb < 10 transfusion threshold (R05) and "rectum always involved in UC" (R13). Where a point needs the book's own statement beside it to be read safely (nasogastric tubes, TIPS timing, albumin with large-volume paracentesis, a single-source terlipressin dose), that statement is printed with it.
  • {{N_REVIEWS}} points for review: the 15 disagreements printed in the Internal Medicine and Other Subjects handouts, the 20 recorded only in the Hepatology ledger, and one found during integration (the transfusion threshold in upper GI bleeding). Two Hepatology points were confirmed on GIT Internal Med p. 43 and now cite both sources. None of them is resolved here, and no disputed value is used as a teaching point.

Traceability

Every entry and review point in this PDF carries an ID (E03.12, R05 …). The merged ledger in the ledger/ folder of the integration branch lists each one with the original handout, the original entry and ledger row IDs, the book and source pages, and what the integration did with it. It also carries every row the three handouts had already excluded, and the reason. The editable source and build instructions are in the same folder.

Limits

  • This handout adds only what the three source handouts found. It does not re-compare the source PDFs with the book from scratch.
  • The Internal Medicine comparison covered book pp. 195–197 of Chapter 15 only. GIT Internal Med has no colorectal-cancer section. Its variceal-bleeding pages (pp. 42–44) were outside that handout's scope; the Hepatology source carries the same text and was compared with Chapter 23.
  • Additions reflect the teaching sources, not a clinical update. Doses and regimens are reproduced as printed and should be taught alongside the book.
