HOW TO USE

About this capsule

A short teaching supplement to YMnotes Digestive System · Version 3. When you finish a chapter, open that chapter here. Each entry adds only what GIT Others contributes beyond your book: a fact, a distinction, a drug or laboratory detail, or a short mechanism. Points your book already makes, even in other words, are left out.

GIT Others p. 20 is the page in GIT others.pdf. Its PDF page number and printed page number are the same.

The sequence continues past intestinal metaplasia: H. pylori gastritis → atrophic gastritis → intestinal metaplasia → dysplasia → cancer.

Foodborn Infection

Microbiology · Internal Medicine · Main book pp. 106–1141st-half file

First-half file, PDF pp. 109–117. GIT Others pp. 22–33 repeat this chapter almost word for word, so only small laboratory and control details are new.

Fits after: Salmonella → Culture and Biochemical reactions · Book p. 107 (1st half PDF p. 110)

Salmonella culture and sugar fermentation

  • A facultative anaerobe that grows well at 37 °C in 24 hours. Colonies on MacConkey are pale (yellow) because it does not ferment lactose.
  • Most species ferment sugars with acid and gas. S. typhi makes acid only (no gas).
MICROBIOLOGYGIT Others p. 22C7-1ledger FI-03, FI-05

Fits after: Enteric fever → Laboratory diagnosis → A. Isolation (first week) · Book p. 109 (1st half PDF p. 112)

How the first-week blood culture is done

Blood is cultured in fluid (broth) media, then subcultured on solid media, mainly MacConkey, at 37 °C.

MICROBIOLOGYGIT Others p. 24C7-2ledger FI-10

Fits after: Enteric fever → Prevention and Control (before “1- Immunization”) · Book p. 109 (1st half PDF p. 112)

Control starts with food handlers

Food handlers are examined to detect carriers. Carriers are treated or kept away from handling food. GIT Others lists this before immunization.

MICROBIOLOGYGIT Others p. 25C7-3ledger FI-16

Fits after: Salmonella food poisoning → A. S. typhimurium · Book p. 110 (1st half PDF p. 113)

S. typhimurium food poisoning is self-limiting

After lasting 2–5 days, it usually resolves on its own (spontaneous cure).

MICROBIOLOGYGIT Others p. 25C7-4ledger FI-18

Fits after: Campylobacter → Laboratory diagnosis · Book p. 111 (1st half PDF p. 114)

Campylobacter laboratory details

  • A rectal swab is an acceptable specimen, collected with care to avoid oxygen exposure.
  • Darting motility is seen in a wet mount under dark-field illumination.
  • Isolated colonies are identified by biotyping, serotyping and phage typing.
MICROBIOLOGYGIT Others p. 27C7-5ledger FI-23

Fits after: Brucella → Culture · Book p. 111 (1st half PDF p. 114)

Brucella needs CO₂

Brucella is aerobic, but culture needs 10% CO₂ as well as serum-enriched media.

MICROBIOLOGYGIT Others p. 27C7-6ledger FI-26

Widal test: what a high H titre means

  • Your book p. 109: a high titre to one H antigen (≥1:160) suggests recent infection with that type; high O titre plus more than one H antigen indicates recent immunization.

Colorectal Cancer

Surgery · Pathology · Main book pp. 195–209Spans both files

Adenocarcinoma: 98% of large-bowel cancers

Your book says “more than 90%” of colorectal carcinomas are adenocarcinomas. GIT Others gives a precise figure: 98% of all cancers of the large intestine are adenocarcinomas.

PATHOLOGYGIT Others p. 19C15-1ledger TU-30

Fits after: 3- Microscopic picture → Mucinous adenocarcinoma (variant) · Book p. 196 (1st half PDF p. 199)

Two kinds of mucoid (mucinous) carcinoma

  • Mucinous adenocarcinoma: abundant extracellular mucin (your book: >50% mucinous material).
  • Signet ring cell carcinoma: the mucin stays inside the malignant cell and pushes the nucleus to one side (eccentric nucleus).
