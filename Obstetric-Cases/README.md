## Fidelity notes
- The source is a scan with no text layer; it was transcribed by hand and checked against 170-dpi renders of every page. The bundled native-text verifier cannot run on scans, so this is a reviewed candidate rather than a natively census-verified release.
- Wording, numbers and Egyptian-Arabic dialogue are kept as printed, including the source's own spellings ("Complain", "لأ؟", "في عند فودافون", "عند 24 سنه").
- **Source inconsistency kept as printed:** the history gives the patient's age as 19 ("عندي 19 سنة") while the Discussion and Summary Table say 18 years old.
- Dropped from the scan: the CamScanner badge, "Page n|6" folios, the heading repeated on every page, and text showing through from the back of the paper.
- Summary-table cells that ran together as " - " lists in the source are shown one item per line; their text is unchanged.
